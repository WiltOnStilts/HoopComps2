What we collect

If you create an account, we store your email, display name, password (encrypted), and collection data you save in the app. We do not collect your eBay username or password.

How we use eBay data

HoopComps uses eBay APIs to show public listing and sold comp information when you scout cards. We use this data only to display market research inside the app. We do not sell eBay data to third parties.
