About HoopComps

Scout basketball card values

What it is

HoopComps is a basketball card value scout built for collectors, flippers, and investors. Enter any card — raw, graded, parallel, or rookie — and pull market comps from eBay, Amazon, and major hobby marketplaces. Track your collection, estimate total portfolio value, compete on the leaderboard, and get AI-powered insights on what you own.

How to use it

  1. Scout — Go to Scout, enter the card title (and player, year, grade if you know them), then tap Scout Market Value. Review sold/active comps, price variation, and market links.
  2. Save — Hit + Add to collection on any scout report to track that card and its estimate.
  3. Collection — See your total portfolio value, refresh all estimates, or run AI collection insight for a full breakdown.
  4. Profile — Set your favorite player and team, create a free account to sync across devices, and opt into the public leaderboard.
  5. Home — Your dashboard shows collection value at a glance, today’s Card of the Day (same spotlight for every user, pulled from featured collectors’ cloud collections), quick actions, and collector rankings.
